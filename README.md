# Jake Jensen

Sports science at Point Loma Nazarene University (M.S. Kinesiology, Biomechanics).
I help lead the biomechanics lab and the baseball analytics team, and I build the tools that turn
motion capture, force plates, TrackMan and high-speed video into reports coaches and players use.

**What's here**
- **seam-tracker** (private, ask me for access): 3D seam orientation from one Edgertronic camera, checked against Qualisys and TrackMan
- **PLNU-Baseball-Player-Report** (private, ask me for access): athlete physical profile report (demo athletes, no real data)
- **cmj-batspeed-transfer**: CMJ height vs. bat speed across competitive levels (regression)
- **mlb-spin-inefficiency-day-vs-night**: spin efficiency in day vs. night MLB games
- **baseball-analytics-scouting-report**: automated MLB hitter scouting reports from Statcast data

**Tools:** Python, Visual3D, Qualisys, TrackMan, VALD, Edgertronic, JavaScript
